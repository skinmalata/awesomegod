Who We Are

Winning souls. Restoring hope. Raising leaders.

Awesome God Ministries exists to win souls back to God, reconcile men to God, and put smiles on the faces of the less privileged — restoring hope to the hopeless and exercising authority in the lives of the sick and the depressed.

Our Mission

To win souls back to God and reconcile men to Him, while putting smiles on the faces of the less privileged — restoring hope to the hopeless through the power of the Gospel.

Our Mandate

Exercising divine authority over sickness and depression, and releasing the wonders and miracles of God — so that every believer experiences the Awesome God personally.

Our Legacy

Founded in 2012 by Apostle Joseph Cookey, the ministry has grown from Port Harcourt to other states — impacting thousands through the Word, the Prophetic School and community empowerment.

The Founder

Apostle Joseph Cookey

General Overseer & Founder of Awesome God Ministries — a highly-anointed man of God, called to the ministry in 2012, known for a ministry of miracles, compassion and generational impact.

Born in Opobo Local Government Area of Rivers State, Apostle Cookey answered the call to serve God and launched Awesome God Ministries in 2012. Mentored by Pastor Sunny Cookey of Lifegate Power Ministries, he is driven by the mandate to win souls, heal the sick and restore hope to the hopeless.

“The mission of Awesome God Ministries is to win souls back to God, reconcile men to God as well as putting smiles on the faces of the less privileged. With this we are restoring hope to the hopeless.” Apostle Joseph Cookey

Happily married to Mrs. Tina Cookey, the Apostle models a life of faith, generosity and family — giving back to widows, orphans and market women across the state as an expression of pure religion before God.

What We Do

Activities

There is a place for every gift, every season and every person at Awesome God Ministries.

Awesome God Prophetic School

Our Bible school equips and raises a generation of pastors and ministers with sound biblical knowledge — impacting nations through trained leaders.

Emergency Demand Urgent Response

Our flagship yearly event — a high-voltage gathering of healings, miracles, signs and wonders where urgent demands receive urgent divine responses.

Remedy — Healing & Health

An arm of the ministry that brings God’s healing touch to the sick, the depressed and the hurting — exercising authority over sickness in the name of Jesus.

Community & Empowerment

From empowering hundreds of market women to Christmas outreach for widows and the less privileged — we live out pure religion by caring for the vulnerable.

The Project

A 10,000-Seat House of God for Port Harcourt

In 2026, Apostle Joseph Cookey officially launched the construction of a striking 10,000-seat auditorium in Port Harcourt — a new landmark that will host thousands of worshippers, community events and city-wide programs, and stand as a major presence in the city’s skyline.
