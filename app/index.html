The Founder

Apostle Joseph Cookey — General Overseer

2012Ministry founded
10,000Seat auditorium

A highly-anointed man of God, called to the ministry in 2012, known for a ministry of miracles, compassion and generational impact.

Born in Opobo Local Government Area of Rivers State, Apostle Cookey launched Awesome God Ministries in 2012, mentored by Pastor Sunny Cookey of Lifegate Power Ministries. He is driven by the mandate to win souls, heal the sick and restore hope to the hopeless.

“The mission of Awesome God Ministries is to win souls back to God, reconcile men to God as well as putting smiles on the faces of the less privileged. With this we are restoring hope to the hopeless.”Apostle Joseph Cookey

Happily married to Mrs. Tina Cookey, he models a life of faith, generosity and family — giving back to widows, orphans and market women across the state.

About the Ministry

Who We Are

Winning souls. Restoring hope. Raising leaders.

Awesome God Ministries exists to win souls back to God, reconcile men to God, and put smiles on the faces of the less privileged — restoring hope to the hopeless and exercising authority in the lives of the sick and the depressed.

Our Mission

To win souls back to God and reconcile men to Him, while putting smiles on the faces of the less privileged — restoring hope to the hopeless through the power of the Gospel.

Our Mandate

Exercising divine authority over sickness and depression, and releasing the wonders and miracles of God — so that every believer experiences the Awesome God personally.

Our Legacy

Founded in 2012 by Apostle Joseph Cookey, the ministry has grown from Port Harcourt to other states — impacting thousands through the Word, the Prophetic School and community empowerment.
